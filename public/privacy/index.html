YOUR INFORMATION

Clarity about
your information.

What this website stores

When you confirm an appointment, the site stores your name, contact detail, chosen support, session language and format, appointment time and status. These details help the practice arrange and manage appointments.

The booking form does not ask for symptoms, a diagnosis or a detailed health history.

Who can access a booking

Authorised practice staff can view and manage bookings in the protected dashboard. Your private appointment link also grants access to your booking, including rescheduling and cancellation. Keep that link private.

Booking information is held in the website’s hosted database. Cancelling changes the appointment status; it does not automatically erase the booking record. Contact the practice about access, corrections or deletion.

Your PHQ-4 answers

Questionnaire answers and scores stay in the open page and are cleared when the check closes. They are not added to a booking, stored in the appointment database or monitored by a clinician.

Messages and calendar files

The enquiry panel prepares text for you to copy. It does not send a message. Contact details are awaiting confirmation. If you later send the text through a messaging service, that provider’s privacy terms apply. Booking confirmations appear on this website; automatic emails and WhatsApp reminders are not currently sent.

Saving an appointment to your calendar creates a file on your device. Your calendar provider’s terms apply if you import or sync it.

Technical information

To limit repeated booking attempts, the site stores an hourly hash of a network address or signed-in identifier and an attempt count. Expired entries are removed during later booking attempts. A change log records booking and schedule actions.

The site code does not include advertising trackers or analytics. The hosting and sign-in services may process technical information under their own policies; those arrangements still need to be included in the practice’s final privacy notice.

Before clinical care begins

This page explains the website’s booking features. Ask the practice for its full clinical privacy notice, record retention arrangements and secure intake process before sharing a detailed health history.

Practice contact details are awaiting confirmation. This notice must be completed with a working privacy contact before public launch.

Need urgent support?

This practice is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, contact the emergency service where you are. In India, call 112 for immediate danger or Tele-MANAS: 14416 for free, 24-hour mental health support. These numbers are for India. Do not wait for a practice reply in an emergency.
