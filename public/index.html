IT’S OKAY TO ASK

Questions
you may have.

Have another question?

Is this website for people outside Kerala and India?

Yes. The information and guides are intended for people of every nationality, culture, faith and background. You do not need a connection to Kerala or India to read them or explore the practice. Clinical appointments depend on availability and the rules where you will be during your session.

Do I need to know what’s wrong?

No. You can begin by describing what has been difficult, what has changed, or what you would like help understanding. You don’t need a diagnosis to make an enquiry.

Can I choose my session language?

You can request English, Malayalam or Hindi. The practice will confirm availability in your preferred language.

Can I have sessions online?

Online care depends on where you will be during the session, the clinician’s professional registration and whether the service is suitable for you. Before booking, ask the practice to confirm availability in your country or region. In-person appointments take place in Kerala, India.

How much does a session cost?

The practice can confirm fees for the service and clinician you’re considering. Ask about session length, assessment costs and cancellation terms before confirming a booking.

What should I know about privacy?

Ask your clinician to explain confidentiality, its limits, record storage and who can access your information before care begins. Use the enquiry form for practical questions. Keep detailed health information for a secure conversation with the practice.

What if the therapist doesn’t feel right for me?

You can raise this with the clinician and discuss a different approach or another referral. The first conversation is also your opportunity to ask whether the therapist and approach are right for you.

Will my family or employer be contacted?

Discuss this directly before care begins. Ask who may receive information, what consent is needed and what exceptions apply. If someone else is arranging or paying for care, clarify their involvement with the clinician.

Can I start if I’m unsure which service I need?

Yes. Choose “Not sure yet” in the enquiry panel. You can ask about an introductory conversation and discuss which clinician or type of support may fit your situation.

When is my appointment confirmed?

Your appointment is confirmed when you select an available time, review your details and press “Confirm appointment.” Save the private link shown afterwards. Confirmation appears on the website; automatic email and WhatsApp messages are not sent. For online care, confirm availability in your location before booking.

    PHQ-4 · ENGLISH · ADULTS 18+

    A brief anxiety & depression screen.

    The Patient Health Questionnaire-4 (PHQ-4) is a published screening instrument studied in adult clinical and general-population samples. It combines two anxiety items (GAD-2) and two depression items (PHQ-2).

    • Answer about the last 2 weeks using the standard response options.
    • This English-language screen is for adults 18+. It cannot diagnose a condition or assess suicide risk.
    • Your answers and scores stay in this page. Closing the check clears them. No clinician monitors or automatically follows up on your result.

    All four answers are needed for a score. You can leave at any time.

    Published total-score bands: normal range 0–2 · mild 3–5 · moderate 6–8 · severe 9–12. These bands describe reported symptom burden, not a diagnosis of depression or anxiety. A low score does not mean that you do not need support.

    What to do next

    Your answers and scores are not included in an enquiry. The practice will not receive this result unless you choose to tell them.

    Research, scoring & source

    Item wording and order follow Appendix 1 of Kroenke et al. (2009). Responses score 0–3; all four items are summed for the 0–12 total. Anxiety uses items 1–2 and depression uses items 3–4. A subscale score of 3 or more is a threshold for further assessment, not a diagnosis.

    • Kroenke et al. (2009): original PHQ-4 clinical study
    • Löwe et al. (2010): general-population validation
    • Published questionnaire and scoring guide

    The evidence is for the questionnaire in studied populations. This website presentation has not been independently clinically validated and does not establish suitability for every language or population.

    Developed by Kurt Kroenke, Robert L. Spitzer, Janet B. W. Williams and Bernd Löwe. PHQ instruments were made available by Pfizer without copyright restriction and at no charge. Reuse information.
