STARTING THERAPY

Questions you can ask before therapy

A first conversation is also a chance to understand your options.

Choose “Save as PDF” in your print options to keep a copy.

You can ask questions before deciding whether a therapist or approach is right for you.

Bring what matters to you.

  • What are your qualifications and experience with this concern?
  • What approach would you suggest, and why?
  • How will we agree goals and review progress?
  • What are the fees and practical arrangements?
  • How are confidentiality and clinical records handled?

You do not need to share your whole history in an enquiry. Ask for clarification when something is unclear, and discuss alternatives if the proposed approach does not feel suitable.

Need urgent support?

This practice is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, contact the emergency service where you are. In India, call 112 for immediate danger or Tele-MANAS: 14416 for free, 24-hour mental health support. These numbers are for India. Do not wait for a practice reply in an emergency.
