Group therapy

A facilitated space to explore common concerns alongside other people. Ask which groups are currently available and how suitability is discussed.

Who it’s for

People interested in working on shared themes in a group setting. Age range, focus and group availability should be confirmed with the practice.

Your first conversation

An initial conversation may explore what you hope to get from the group and explain participation, confidentiality and expectations before you join.

Useful questions to bring

  • Group focus, age range and size.
  • Facilitator qualifications and how group suitability is assessed.
  • Dates, fees, attendance expectations and confidentiality agreements.

Need urgent support?

This practice is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, contact the emergency service where you are. In India, call 112 for immediate danger or Tele-MANAS: 14416 for free, 24-hour mental health support. These numbers are for India. Do not wait for a practice reply in an emergency.
